# Research note template

Every file in `research/` answers these nine headings, in this order. Nothing else.

## 1. What they do
## 2. Why it works
## 3. What they do badly
## 4. What we should copy conceptually
## 5. What we can do better
## 6. What is technically required
## 7. What could break
## 8. What we can uniquely do because of Nimiq
## 9. Licence and reuse verdict

Rules:
- Every factual claim carries a source URL, or is marked **NOT VERIFIED**.
- Licences are recorded exactly as the repository states them (file name + SPDX id), never guessed.
- No feature is recommended without saying what it costs and what it risks.
